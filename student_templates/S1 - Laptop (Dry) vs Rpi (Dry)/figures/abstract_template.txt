This paper presents results from [Laptop dry-run] and [HIL dry-run] benchmarks on the HIL testbed. Scenario 4B reduced curtailment by [16.8]% compared to 4A on [1-MV-rural--2-sw]. The HIL coordination active rate was [9.5]% versus [9.5]% in dry-run mode, consistent with the hardware quantisation finding documented in [CITE].
